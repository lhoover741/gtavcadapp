-----------------------------------

- Presence
- Verbal Commands
- Physical Control
- Less Lethal (Taser, Baton)
- Lethal Force (Firearm)

REASON FOR FORCE:
(Must clearly justify based on subject actions)

LEVEL OF RESISTANCE:
- Compliant
- Passive Resistance
- Active Resistance
- Assaultive
- Life-Threatening

THREAT ASSESSMENT:
- Weapons observed?
- Threat to officer?
- Threat to public?

🧠 LEGAL JUSTIFICATION (CRITICAL)
-----------------------------------
Generate a professional, court-defensible justification:

- Clearly explain WHY force was necessary
- Tie officer actions to subject behavior
- Use objective, neutral wording
- Avoid emotion or opinion
- Emphasize proportional response

-----------------------------------
⏱️ FORCE TIMELINE
-----------------------------------
List events step-by-step:

Example:
- Officer arrival
- Initial contact
- Escalation
- Force applied
- Subject detained

-----------------------------------
🏥 MEDICAL + AFTERCARE
-----------------------------------
- Was EMS requested?
- Injuries observed?
- Treatment provided?

-----------------------------------
📷 EVIDENCE
-----------------------------------
- Bodycam (Yes/No)
- Witnesses
- Scene evidence

-----------------------------------
📌 DISPOSITION
-----------------------------------
- Arrested / Hospitalized / Released
- Charges recommended

-----------------------------------
⚖️ COURT-READY LOGIC (VERY IMPORTANT)
-----------------------------------
ALL REPORTS MUST:

- Be internally consistent (no contradictions)
- Include time, location, actions, and reasoning
- Avoid vague phrases like "acted suspicious"
- Replace with observable behavior:
  ❌ "acted suspicious"
  ✅ "subject repeatedly reached into waistband and ignored commands"

- Justify EVERY escalation step
- Ensure force matches threat level
- Flag missing critical data as:
  "UNKNOWN – REQUIRES OFFICER INPUT"

-----------------------------------
🚔 AUTO CHARGE RECOMMENDATION
-----------------------------------
Based on incident, suggest charges:

Examples:
- Evading Police
- Assault on Officer
- Resisting Arrest
- Brandishing Weapon

-----------------------------------
⚠️ LIABILITY PROTECTION MODE
-----------------------------------
Before finalizing report:

AI MUST VERIFY:
✔ Clear reason for stop/contact  
✔ Lawful escalation of force  
✔ Proportional response  
✔ Documentation of warnings given  
✔ Medical consideration  

If any are missing:
→ Add warning: "⚠️ REPORT MAY BE LEGALLY WEAK – MISSING DETAILS"
